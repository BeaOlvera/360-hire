## 1. Who we are

Zephyron Consulting operates the 360 Hire platform. Contact: bolvera.arias8@gmail.com.

**Our role depends on whose data it is:**

| Whose data | Our role |
|---|---|
| Candidates assessed through the platform | **Processor**, acting on the instructions of the hiring organisation, which is the controller |

We are also the **provider** of a high-risk AI system under Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, classified under Annex III point 4(a).

## 2. Candidate data — where we act as processor

We process candidate data only on the documented instructions of the client organisation that invited the candidate.

**If you are a candidate:** the organisation you applied to is responsible for your data, and their privacy notice governs. If you contact us directly we will forward your request to them and tell you we have done so, normally within five working days. We will not act on a request about candidate data without the controller's instruction, because we are not entitled to.

## 3. Sub-processors and international transfers

The platform relies on: **Anthropic** (AI analysis), **OpenAI** (speech-to-text), **Supabase** (database and file storage), **Vercel** (hosting), **Resend** (email).

All are established in the **United States**. Candidate data — including CV content, transcripts, voice and video — is transferred outside the EEA in the ordinary course of processing. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

We do not sell personal data, and no sub-processor uses candidate data to train models.

## 5. Security

TLS in transit and encryption at rest; private object storage with short-lived signed URLs; service-role credentials confined to server-side code; token-scoped candidate access; audit logging of access to transcripts and recordings.

Measures still outstanding are recorded openly in ROPA §3 and tracked in the risk management system, rather than omitted from this policy.

## 6. Our AI transparency commitments

| Commitment | Detail |
|---|---|
| Candidates are told they are interacting with an AI | AI Act Art. 50, in force since 2 August 2026 |
| **We do not infer emotions** from face, voice or expression | The video recording is never sent to any model. No vision API exists in the platform. Verified by code review; recorded at Technical Documentation §2. |
| **We do not make hiring decisions** | The system produces a recommendation. A human at the client organisation decides. No candidate is rejected, filtered or ranked out without a person acting. |
| We do not claim validated accuracy | No accuracy metric has yet been established for this system, and we will not assert one until testing supports it. |
| We tell clients before we change anything material | Models, instruments, scoring, oversight design or sub-processors |
